Government Schemes & Official Information
Farmer Help

Farmers can also seek agriculture-related assistance through official government portals and local agriculture department/CSC services. Always verify current eligibility and application requirements on the official portal before applying.

How to Use Farmer Support

Follow these simple steps to find a suitable scheme or agricultural support service.

1
Identify Your Need

Choose the problem you need help with, such as income support, crop insurance, soil health, credit or irrigation.

2
Select a Scheme

Review the government schemes below and choose the one that matches your requirement.

3
Check Eligibility

Read the eligibility rules and current requirements on the official government portal.

4
Keep Documents Ready

Keep the required identity, land, bank and crop-related documents ready when applicable.

5
Open Official Website

Use the official information button on the selected scheme card and avoid unofficial application links.

6
Apply & Check Status

Submit the application through the official channel and keep your application/reference number for status checks.

Problems Identified from Survey
  • Lack of awareness of government schemes — 12 farmers (60%)
  • Water Scarcity — 5 farmers (25%)
  • High Input Cost — 4 farmers (20%)
  • Pests and Diseases — 4 farmers (20%)
  • Low Market Price — 3 farmers (15%)
  • Lack of Irrigation — 2 farmers (10%)
  • Lack of Technical Knowledge — 2 farmers (10%)
Recommended CSP Actions
  • Conduct a government-scheme awareness session using the Farmer Support section.
  • Explain where farmers can find official scheme information and status/application services.
  • Promote water-efficient irrigation and crop-specific water management.
  • Share practical guidance on input management, pest monitoring and market awareness.
  • Collect farmer feedback after awareness activities and compare before/after awareness levels.
